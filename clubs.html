Discover More About Student Clubs

What happens in a technical club?

Technical clubs organise coding competitions, hackathons, workshops, technical events, and collaborative projects.

Can beginners join student clubs?

Yes! Most clubs welcome beginners and provide opportunities to learn new skills through activities and workshops.

How can I become a club member?

Students can participate in recruitment drives, attend club events, and follow official announcements.

Why Join a Club?

  1. Meet students with similar interests.
  2. Practise teamwork and communication.
  3. Build practical skills outside the classroom.
  4. Take part in campus events and projects.
