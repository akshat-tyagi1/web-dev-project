Explore Different Communities

Here are examples of student communities and the interests they represent. Check the university's official club directory for current details and recruitment announcements.

CSI — Computer Society of India

A community for students interested in computing, technology, programming, and professional development.

ACM — Association for Computing Machinery

A computing-focused student community where members can explore technical learning, problem-solving, and collaborative projects.

PULSE

For students interested in organising events, coordinating activities, and contributing to campus experiences.

ADVAITA

A space for students interested in music, performance, and musical collaboration.

RIVAAZ

A community for students who enjoy dance, choreography, and stage performances.

BU Gamers

For students interested in gaming, friendly competition, and gaming communities.

Why Join a Club?

  1. Meet students with similar interests.
  2. Practise teamwork and communication.
  3. Build practical skills outside the classroom.
  4. Take part in campus events and projects.
