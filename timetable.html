Plan Your Work

  1. Write down every assignment and its deadline.
  2. Break large tasks into smaller steps.
  3. Start with urgent or high-priority work.
  4. Review your progress at the end of the week.

Remember to Make Time For

  • Sleep and regular meals
  • Exercise and downtime
  • Friends and campus activities
  • Revision before assessments

Tip: A planner is useful only if you update it regularly and keep the tasks realistic.
